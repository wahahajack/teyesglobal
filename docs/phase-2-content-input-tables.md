## 7. Australia Site Reuse Checklist

Use AU site materials only after checking:

- Is the information global or Australia-specific?
- Is pricing/shipping/warranty language local-only?
- Is the product model still current?
- Is the fitment guide relevant outside Australia?
- Does the content need to be rewritten for distributors/installers instead of retail buyers?
- Does it need approval from TEYES or the site owner before reuse?

Priority reusable topics:

- Comparison specification charts.
- CC4 Pro vs CC3 2K.
- CC4L vs CC4 vs CC4 Pro.
- Installation videos.
- CANBUS settings.
- Firmware guidance.
- Common faults and solutions.
- OEM head unit pinouts.
- Brochures.
- FAQ structure.
