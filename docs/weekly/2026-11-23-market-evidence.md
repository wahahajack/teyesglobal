# TEYESGLOBAL weekly evidence record — 16–23 November 2026

Research cutoff: 23 November 2026 (Asia/Shanghai). Primary sources were preferred. This record separates confirmed facts, vendor claims, secondary observations and inference.

## Confirmed developments

### European Union — CRA guidance reaches the Official Journal and first supporting standards arrive

- **27 July guidance / 17 November 2026 Official Journal publication — confirmed, European Commission.** The Commission issued non-binding guidance on Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 in July; it appeared as OJ C/2026/6013 on 17 November. For automotive products, the guidance says the CRA exclusion applies to components exclusively designed and constructed for integration into vehicles covered by Regulations (EU) 2019/2144 or 168/2013 and clearly suitable only for that integration. It treats generic digital components not exclusively suitable for those vehicles as within CRA scope. Distribution through general retail or online channels open to the public is evidence against the automotive exclusion; a restricted B2B automotive channel may indicate exclusivity but is not, by itself, a blanket exemption. Sources: Commission announcement, 27 July 2026: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/commission-publishes-new-guidance-support-timely-cyber-resilience-act-implementation ; stable OJ guidance: https://data.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/6013/oj
- **18–19 November 2026 — confirmed, European Commission and CEN-CENELEC.** CEN and CENELEC published EN 40000-1-1 (vocabulary) and EN 40000-1-2 (principles, product risk management and lifecycle activities). EN 40000-1-2 covers design, development, production, maintenance, end of support and decommissioning. More detailed vulnerability-handling, generic-security and product-category standards are still in development. Sources: European Commission, published 19 November 2026: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/first-european-standard-published-support-cyber-resilience-act ; CEN-CENELEC, published 19 November 2026: https://www.cencenelec.eu/news-events/news/2026/en-in-the-spotlight/2026-11-19-cra-standards-en-40000-1-1-and-en-40000-1-2/
- **Confirmed background, not new this week.** CRA reporting obligations have applied since 11 September 2026; the main product obligations apply from 11 December 2027. Importers of in-scope products must verify the manufacturer's product requirements, vulnerability-handling processes, conformity assessment, technical documentation, EU declaration and CE marking. Sources: European Commission CRA summary: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-summary ; reporting page: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting ; legal text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R2847
- **Confirmed legal boundary.** Article 27 presumption of conformity depends on references to harmonised standards being published in the Official Journal. No such OJEU reference for EN 40000-1-1 or EN 40000-1-2 was identified by the 23 November cutoff. Availability of the standards is not itself a product certificate or an automatic presumption of conformity.
- **Commercial inference.** EU importers of broadly marketed connected aftermarket head units now have a clearer reason to request a documented product-scope assessment and lifecycle evidence by exact hardware/software configuration. The new standard is a process framework, not a blanket “CRA certificate” for a head unit.

### United States and Canada — vehicle-integration module update

- **17 November announcement / 19 November publication — vendor announcement reported by trade press.** Automotive Data Solutions announced the Maestro ADS-MRR3 as the MRR2 replacement. Claimed changes include a faster MCU, six configurable outputs, a 25% smaller enclosure and Bluetooth programming; it is planned to ship to ADS distributors and authorised retailers in early December at the MRR2 price. Source: CEoutlook, 19 November 2026: https://www.ceoutlook.com/2026/11/19/automotive-data-solutions-inc-ads-announces-the-official-release-of-the-maestro-ads-mrr3/
- **20 November — dealer/installer observation, secondary.** 12 Volt Outlet lists US$319.99 and stresses that exact vehicle, trim, radio, harness and firmware checks remain necessary. Source: https://www.12voltoutlet.com/blogs/car-audio-news-12v-industry-updates/idatalink-maestro-rr3-ads-mrr3-radio-replacement-interface
- **Commercial inference.** North American dealers continue to value programmable integration and compact packaging as vehicles retain more factory functions. TEYES should not imply Maestro compatibility without product-level evidence; the relevant response is a configuration-specific CAN/function-retention matrix and installation pack.

### United States — premium factory-retention interface

- **17 November 2026 — vendor announcement through trade press.** RDV Automotive Technology announced wireless CarPlay/Android Auto interfaces for 11 McLaren models, with factory-control retention, front/rear camera inputs and a three-year warranty. Source: CEoutlook: https://www.ceoutlook.com/2026/11/17/rdvfl-brings-wireless-carplay-and-wireless-android-auto-to-mclaren/
- **Commercial inference.** At the high end, factory-retention interfaces remain an alternative to full radio replacement. The warranty length is a channel-confidence benchmark, but the niche launch does not justify a TEYES article by itself.

Official specifications: https://www.alpine.co.uk/p/Products/SingleView/ute-301bt
