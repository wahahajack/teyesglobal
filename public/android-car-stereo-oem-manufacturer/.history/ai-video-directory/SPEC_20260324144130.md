# AI Video Generation Models Directory - Specification

## Concept & Vision

A sleek, modern directory showcasing the best AI video generation models available in 2024-2025. The website feels like stepping into a futuristic control room—dark, sophisticated, with glowing accents that evoke the cutting-edge nature of AI technology. Each tool is presented as a "station" in a gallery of innovation, inviting exploration.

## Design Language

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark mode cyberpunk-meets-minimalism aesthetic. Think Tron meets Apple—sophisticated darkness with precise, glowing highlights. Cards float with subtle depth, and interactions feel smooth and responsive.

### Color Palette
- **Primary Background**: `#0a0a0f` (deep space black)
- **Secondary Background**: `#12121a` (card surfaces)
