Manufacturing FAQs

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

For standard products (Series manufacturing), MOQ is 100 units. For OEM branding (custom packaging), MOQ starts at 500 units. For full ODM hardware customization, typically 3,000+ units. We can discuss options for strategic partnerships with lower volumes.

How quickly can you deliver samples?

Standard samples: 2-4 weeks. Expedited samples: 1-2 weeks with rush fee. Full production orders: 45-90 days depending on volume and customization level.

Do you offer OEM/ODM customization?

Yes. We provide OEM branding (logos, packaging) and ODM services (custom UI, firmware, hardware modifications). Our engineering team works with you from concept to mass production.

What certifications do you support?

We hold ISO 9001, IATF 16949, CE, FCC, RoHS, and support regional certifications (ANATEL Brazil, NOM Mexico, etc). We help with documentation, testing coordination, and compliance verification.

Can I visit the factory and verify production?

Absolutely. We welcome factory audits and visits. We can arrange facility tours, show production lines live, provide third-party audit reports (SGS, TÜV), and conduct video calls with our production team.

What payment terms do you offer?

Flexible terms: 50% deposit + 50% on delivery for new customers. Other arrangements available for established partnerships (30/70, L/C, etc). We prioritize secure, mutually beneficial terms.
