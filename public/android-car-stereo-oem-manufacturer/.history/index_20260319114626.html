Car Stereo Factory & Supply FAQs

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

For standard products, MOQ starts from 10 units depending on model and configuration. For OEM branding such as packaging, logo, and UI presets, MOQ usually starts from 500 units. Deeper ODM or hardware programs normally require larger volume planning.

How quickly can you deliver samples?

Standard samples are typically ready in about 1 week. Standard production orders can often be delivered within about 2 weeks after confirmation. OEM and ODM timelines depend on the exact customization scope.

Is OEM/ODM available if we need customization?

Yes. We support OEM branding, UI presets, software-related customization, and project-based development discussions. Many buyers start with standard supply first and move into OEM or ODM after validating the market.

What certifications do you support?

We hold ISO 9001, IATF 16949, CE, FCC, and RoHS, and we also support regional certification discussions such as ANATEL and NOM. We can help with documentation review, testing coordination, and compliance planning.

Can I visit the factory and verify production?

Yes. We welcome factory visits, live facility reviews, and third-party audits. If travel is not convenient, we can also arrange video verification and direct communication with the production team.

What payment terms do you offer?

For new customers, common terms are 50% deposit and 50% before delivery. Different arrangements can be discussed for established partnerships and larger programs based on order history and project scope.
