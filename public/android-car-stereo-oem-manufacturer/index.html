Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify you are a real factory, not a trader?

We welcome factory audits at our 50,000㎡ facility in Shenzhen. We can provide: business license, factory video tour, third-party audit reports (SGS, TÜV), and live video calls showing our production lines in real-time.

What is the MOQ for OEM manufacturing?

For standard OEM branding (logo + packaging), MOQ starts at 500 units. For full custom hardware, MOQ is typically 3,000+ units. This ensures only serious buyers — we don't do trial orders of 10 units for OEM projects.

How long from concept to first shipment?

Standard branding: 6–8 weeks. ODM (software customization): 3–4 months. Full custom hardware with new tooling: 8–12 months including certification.

Can I customize both hardware and software?

Yes. We offer full-stack customization: hardware design, Android OS, UI/UX, app integration, boot logo, and complete branding. You choose the level — from simple logo swap to ground-up design.

What certifications can TEYES help with?

We support CE, FCC, E-Mark, RoHS, and regional certifications (ANATEL, NOM, etc). Our team handles documentation and coordinates with testing labs.

Do you provide after-sales technical support?

Yes. OEM partners receive firmware updates, technical documentation, remote diagnostics, and a dedicated project manager. We stand behind every unit we build.
